Collection preview, test checkout and our conservation pledge

By Project Suricate · Last substantive update: November 5, 2026

Project Suricate is in collection preview. Real sales are not open. The checkout is a sandbox for testing, not a way to place a real order.

What is Project Suricate?

A wildlife-inspired apparel project developing graphic tees and hoodies for animal lovers, with a conservation-focused mission.

Which designs are being previewed?

  • Siberian Tiger Tee
  • Hawksbill Sea Turtle Hoodie
  • Snow Leopard Tee
  • Axolotl Tee
  • Blue Whale Hoodie
  • Mountain Gorilla Tee

These are design concepts, not final merchandise or product photography. Explore the collection preview.

Can I buy something today?

No. The separate Stripe test cart supports designs, sizes and quantities for testing. Test checkout does not create real charges, orders, shipments or conservation donations.

What does the conservation pledge mean?

The pledge is to allocate 50% of net profits to conservation donations. Net profit means what remains after business costs; this is not a pledge to donate half of the full purchase price.

Who are the planned donation recipients?

The total donation goal is $10,000: $3,000 for Rainforest Trust, $3,000 for Center for Biological Diversity, $2,000 for Wild Ones, and $2,000 for Conserve Wildlife Foundation of New Jersey. These are planned recipients, not claimed partners or endorsements. The goal is not a statement of money already raised or donated.

Where are the financial reports?

Financial reports and donation receipts are not published yet. The reporting schedule is being finalized. Verified totals should be supported by records when published; no verified donation total is claimed in this update.

What comes next?

Final artwork, product details and real-order readiness remain future milestones. Updates will document meaningful developments as they happen, without announcing a launch date that has not been set.
